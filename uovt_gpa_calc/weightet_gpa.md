In the University of Vocational Technology grading system, a subject's weight in the GPA calculation is determined strictly by its assigned **Credit Value**. Modules designated as Non-GPA (NG) carry a weight of zero and do not impact the final score.

For any given subject, the calculation is applied as follows:
$Weighted\ Score=Grade\ Point\times Credit\ Value$

This report outlines the standard grading methodology and GPA calculation formulas applied across all subjects in the Software Technology, Multimedia & Web Technology, and Network Technology streams.

**Course Assessment and Eligibility**

* Continuous assessments account for **30% - 40%** of the module grade, while the semester-end examination accounts for **60% - 70%**.


* Students must secure at least **30%** in continuous assessments to qualify for the end-semester exam.


* Weekday students require **80%** attendance, while weekend students require **60%** attendance to sit for examinations.


* Credits are only earned for a module if a student achieves a grade of **D** or above.


* Only Core Compulsory modules and Elective modules labeled as **GPA modules (G)** are factored into the GPA calculations; Non-GPA modules (NG) do not impact the final score.

**Semester GPA (SGPA) Calculation**
The SGPA determines a student's standing for a specific semester and is rounded to the second decimal place. The formula is calculated as follows:

$$SGPA = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(c_i \times g_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}c_i}$$

* $c_i$ is the number of credits for the $i^{th}$ module.


* $g_i$ is the Grade Points earned for that module.


* $n$ is the total number of modules in the respective Semester.



**Final GPA (FGPA) Calculation**
The FGPA represents the absolute academic standing across the entire degree program and determines the final degree classification.

$$Final\ GPA = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}(c_i \times g_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N}c_i}$$

* $c_i$ is the number of credits for the $i^{th}$ module.


* $g_i$ is the Grade Points earned for that module.


* $N$ is the total number of modules in the respective Degree Programme.
